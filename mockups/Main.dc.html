Grandma's Apple Tart

The recipe my grandmother made every Sunday in autumn — a thin buttery crust, soft cinnamon apples, and just enough sugar to let the fruit speak for itself.

Method

1

In a large bowl, rub the cold butter into the flour with your fingertips until it looks like coarse breadcrumbs. Add the sugar, then the cold water a tablespoon at a time until the dough just comes together.

2

Shape into a disc, wrap, and chill for at least 30 minutes. Meanwhile, peel and slice the apples thinly, then toss with the brown sugar, cinnamon and vanilla.

3

Roll the chilled dough out on a floured surface and line a 24cm tart tin. Arrange the apple slices in overlapping circles, working from the outside in.

4

Bake at 190°C for 40–45 minutes until the crust is golden and the apples are tender at the edges. Let it cool for 10 minutes before slicing.
